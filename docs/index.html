What it is

FocusTube is a tiny desktop window for one job: play the video and get out of your way. It boots in a corner, stays above everything, and never shows you the rest of YouTube.

🔝

Always on top

Stays above every other window so the video follows you across apps. Pin it anywhere.

🧹

Borderless & clean

No title bar, no sidebar, no comments. Just the player, sized your way.

🎚️

Adjustable opacity

Drag the transparency slider to peek through the video while you type or read behind it.

⌨️

Keyboard control

Space pauses and plays — even when FocusTube isn't the active window.

Tips & Tricks

Get the most out of FocusTube with these pro tips.

01

Transparency peek

Drag the opacity slider in the title bar to make the video semi-transparent. Great for following along with code tutorials while typing.

02

Global play/pause

FocusTube registers a global shortcut — Space plays or pauses even when the app isn't focused. Perfect for music or podcasts.

03

Corner snap

Resize the window small and drag it to a corner of your primary monitor. It stays above everything, so your video is always visible.

04

Media keys work

Your keyboard's play/pause media key controls FocusTube too — even when another app is active. No extra setup needed.

05

Multiple instances

Run multiple FocusTube windows to watch several videos at once (great for monitoring streams or comparing tutorials).

06

No account needed

FocusTube uses the YouTube IFrame API — you don't need to sign in to Google or YouTube. Your watch history stays private.

FAQ

Common questions about FocusTube.

Do I need a YouTube account or API key?

No. FocusTube uses the same YouTube IFrame API that works in any browser. Paste any public YouTube URL and it plays. No account, no API key, no sign-in.

Does FocusTube show ads?

No. FocusTube embeds videos using the standard YouTube player, which may show pre-roll or overlay ads depending on the video. There are no ads, sponsored content, or tracking injected by FocusTube itself.

Does it work with YouTube Music or playlists?

FocusTube loads single videos. YouTube Music URLs and playlists aren't supported yet — paste a direct video link and it plays.

Can I watch live streams?

Yes — live YouTube URLs work the same as recorded videos. Just paste the link and the stream loads.

Is FocusTube open source?

Yes. The entire source code is on GitHub under an open source license. Contributions, issues, and feature requests are welcome.

Does it collect any data?

Zero. FocusTube has no analytics, no telemetry, no tracking, and no ads. It never connects to any server except YouTube's embedded player (which needs to fetch the video). The app never phones home.
